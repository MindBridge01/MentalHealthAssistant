MINDBRIDGE · PROFESSIONAL SETUP

Your profile and appointment guide

1. Review your approved details

Sign in and open the professional dashboard. Your verified name, professional type and qualifications appear automatically. Contact the administrator to correct credentials; these are not editable public-profile fields.

2. Complete your introduction

Add your biography, services, consultation languages and patient age groups. Keep claims factual and within your professional scope. Never put patient information in your public profile.

3. Arrange your hospital affiliation

The administrator verifies the arrangement offline and links your account to a hospital or clinic. The hospital provides the booking officer, address, payment instructions and public contact number. Demo organisations must be labelled as demonstrations.

4. Publish and create slots

Complete the profile, enable publishing and accepting requests. Select a linked hospital and enter each appointment's start and end, display timezone and fee or contact-clinic instruction. The time inputs use your device timezone. Only offer slots agreed with the hospital. Overlapping slots are blocked across locations.

5. Understand requests

A request is pending until the hospital booking officer confirms it. The officer can decline or propose another available time. The patient must accept a proposal. Unanswered requests expire after 24 hours or at the appointment time. Confirmed appointments receive reminders within 24 hours of the appointment.

6. Payment and changes

Payments, receipts and refunds are handled directly by the hospital. MindBridge does not collect payment or infer that payment was made. Cancellation updates are emailed. Cancel existing requests before removing their slots. Turning off new bookings does not cancel existing appointments.
